1:25 pm
Land at LJU · pick up rental car
~20 min drive into the city center. Check in, drop bags.
~3 pm
Ljubljana old town walk high priority
Dragon Bridge → Triple Bridge → Central Market (check Sunday hours) → Cooperative Business Bank Building (fairy-tale art nouveau, Slovenian tricolour facade) → Ljubljana Castle by funicular for panoramic views over the city.
~5:30 pm
Park Špica river loop
Walk from the city center down the Ljubljanica to Špica Park — there's a kayak racing course in the middle of the city. Cross at Prule Bridge, walk the other bank back. ~4 km, beautiful evening light on the river.
~7:30 pm
Dinner: TaBar friend rec high priority
Slovenian tapas in the old town, Ribji Trg square. Must-order: roast octopus with polenta. Sit outside. Recommended by both Michelle and Jake independently — a strong signal.
After dinner
Drinks: Vinoteka Movia → Tozd
Start at Vinoteka Movia for Slovenian orange wines and cosy dark interiors. Move on to Tozd for craft beers by the river in their signature orange mugs, with a library of books to browse.
If the night gets rowdy: Burek Olimpija is open almost 24 hours — the cheese pastry is the order.

Monday · May 26
Ljubljana → Soča Valley
Sleep: Kobarid/Bovec area
▾
Morning
Pekarna Ana breakfast high priority
Ana Roš's bakery — sourdough, cruffins, biscuits by the former head baker of Hiša Franko. The perfect send-off from Ljubljana before hitting the road.
~9 am
Drive to Zelenci Nature Reserve high priority
Striking emerald-green springs where the Sava river begins — ethereally clear water over a white sandy bed. Short easy walk. A visual jaw-dropper that takes about 45 minutes total.
~1.5h from Ljubljana
~11:30 am
Arrive Kobarid · Kozjak Waterfall hike hike high priority
Easy ~1.5h return hike through a dramatic gorge along the turquoise Soča. The town itself carries Hemingway history — the 1917 Battle of Caporetto retreat he documented in A Farewell to Arms.
Zelenci → Kobarid ~45min
~2 pm
Late lunch: Topli Val high priority
45-year-old seafood institution in the heart of the Alps. Fish from Slovenian Adriatic fishermen, trout from local farms, game from local hunters. Three generations of the Hvala family. Sip Chardonnay on the terrace. Fish carpaccio is the standout. Take your time — you're in no rush.
Late afternoon
Drive the Soča Valley road high priority
One of the most scenic drives in Europe — turquoise river through Alpine gorges. Stop at viewpoints. Bovec is ~30 min north if you want to swim in the Soča. Then settle in for the night near Kobarid.
Evening
Light dinner or drinks near accommodation
Tomorrow is Hiša Franko lunch — €335 tasting menu, ~3 hours. Keep tonight light. Local wine, cheese, charcuterie from a shop. Save your appetite.

Tuesday · May 27
Hiša Franko → Brda → Bled
Sleep: Bled area
▾
Morning
Slow morning in the valley
Walk along the Soča, coffee in Kobarid. Hiša Franko is a farmhouse restaurant — arrive relaxed, not rushed.
Lunch
Hiša Franko ★★★ michelin high priority
Ana Roš's 3-Michelin-star restaurant. 2026 menu "Footprints" — €335 pp, ~3 hours, beverages extra. Spring menu tells the story of the valley through dishes that evolve constantly with what nature provides each week. Watch the Chef's Table episode beforehand if you haven't.
Budget ~3.5 hours including arrival and lingering. Finish around 4–4:30 pm. Wine pairing will add significantly — plan accordingly.
~4:30 pm
Brda wine region · MOVIA winery high priority
~45 min from Kobarid toward Bled. Rolling hills on the Italian border — one of Slovenia's most beautiful landscapes. MOVIA: family since 1820, biodynamic, famous for Puro sparkling wine and Rebula whites. Call ahead to check tasting availability. Burja Estate is nearby for a second stop if you have energy.
Kobarid → Brda ~45min · Brda → Bled ~1h
Evening
Arrive Bled · Dinner: Domačija Majer'ca friend rec high priority
Michelle's #1 recommendation — "genuinely one of the best meals, elegant yet homey log cabin, incredibly nice servers." Newish restaurant near Lake Bled, also a guesthouse. Stay here if they have rooms — waking up here after that meal would be a treat.
4
Wednesday · May 28
Bled → Bohinj → Ljubljana
Sleep: Ljubljana
▾
Morning
Lake Bled friend rec high priority
Walk or cycle the 6 km lake path while it's still quiet. Paddle boat to Bled Island — rent your own rather than a tour boat (Michelle's strong advice: more private, same price roughly). Visit the baroque Church of the Mother of God, ring the wishing bell from 1534. Kremšnita cream cake at a lakeside café is non-negotiable.
~10:30 am
Bled Castle
Panoramic views over the lake and Julian Alps. Medieval castle perched on a sheer cliff — genuinely dramatic. Takes about an hour.
~12 pm
Lake Bohinj · Triglav National Park hike high priority
~30 min from Bled. Less famous, less crowded, more wild. Gateway to Triglav National Park — deep gorges, alpine meadows, crystal-clear rivers. Walk around the lake or hike into the park. Keep eyes open for red deer, chamois, golden eagles.
Bled → Bohinj ~30min
~2:30 pm
Drive back to Ljubljana
Check in. Afternoon wander — Tivoli Park, Emporium Gallery, or just sit by the river with a coffee at Kavarna Zvezda.
~1.5h
Evening
Dinner: Grič high priority
Chef Luka Košir's daily tasting menu in a village just outside Ljubljana. Eco-certified duck farm since 2013, own vegetable gardens and orchards, foraged ingredients, hyper-local. Wood-panelled ceiling, homely tables, natural light. The right final dinner: not as overwhelming as another 3-star, but genuinely special and very Slovenian.
Backup if Grič is fully booked: Gostilna na Gradu inside Ljubljana Castle (great seasonal tasting menu, incredible views over the city).
Late night
Tozd + Burek Olimpija send-off
Craft beers and cold brew by the river at Tozd. If it gets late and hungry: cheese burek at Olimpija, open nearly 24 hours.

~20min
Hiša Franko — book immediately

Book lunch on May 27. You're already sleeping in the Soča Valley on the night of May 26, so you arrive at the restaurant relaxed, on the right day, in daylight. A farmhouse surrounded by Alpine scenery deserves to be experienced in the afternoon sun, not finished at 10 pm in the dark. The meal ends around 4–4:30 pm, leaving a perfect window to drive through the Brda wine region en route to Bled.

At €335 pp with beverages on top, budget roughly €500–550 pp for the full experience with wine pairing. Book at hisafranko.com — this is the single most time-sensitive booking of the trip.

Where to stay each night

Night 1 (May 25): Ljubljana city center — anywhere walkable to the old town.

Night 2 (May 26): Kobarid or Bovec area. Keep it simple — you're eating lightly and need to be fresh for Hiša Franko the next day. A farmhouse stay or small hotel in Kobarid works perfectly.

Night 3 (May 27): Domačija Majer'ca doubles as a guesthouse — try to book a room there so you're already on-site after dinner. If unavailable, Michelle stayed at Domacija Log v Bohinju (between two mountains, family-owned estate, welcoming host) — also excellent.

Night 4 (May 28): Ljubljana city center again — same area as Night 1, or the same hotel if you want simplicity.

What to book in advance

1. Hiša Franko lunch · May 27 — book today, hisafranko.com

2. Domačija Majer'ca · dinner + room · May 27 — newish, popular, likely fills up

3. Grič · dinner · May 28 — tasting menu restaurants in Slovenia book up weeks ahead

4. MOVIA winery · May 27 afternoon — call ahead, most Brda wineries require reservations for tastings

5. TaBar · May 25 — less critical but worth a reservation for three people on a Sunday evening

Didn't make the itinerary — and why
These are great recommendations that simply don't fit a western-focused 4-day loop. Most warrant a return trip.
Ljubljana extras
Strelec, JB Restaurant, Gostilna As, Vigò, Lolita, Pop's Place, Kavarna Zvezda, Museum of Modern Art, MAO, Cooperative Bank Building — great for an extra Ljubljana day. Vigò (pistachio ice cream), Lolita (hot chocolate by the river), and Pop's (burgers) are all easy daytime additions on Day 1 if you're not too tired from the flight. JB is listed as temporarily closed — verify before planning around it.
East Slovenia
Opok27, Hiša Denk, Terme Olimia, Hotel Laško spa — the Štajerska region is 2+ hours from the western circuit and in completely the opposite direction. Both Opok27 and Hiša Denk are exceptional destination restaurants. Hiša Denk has guest rooms from ~$100/night. This region warrants its own dedicated trip.
Southwest Karst
Škocjan Caves, Predjama Castle, Agriturismo Nema, Pr'Pepet, Hiša Polona, Gostilna pri Lojzetu — the Karst/Vipava Valley area is a full day-circuit on its own heading south from Ljubljana. Gostilna pri Lojzetu (17th-century manor, Tomaž Kavčič's deconstructionist Vipava cuisine) is a genuine destination restaurant. Predjama Castle (world's largest cave castle) is spectacular but ~1h from Ljubljana — adds risk to the May 29 morning flight.
Brda extras
EDI SIMČIČ Winery, Cafe Čokl, :PROJEKT Beershop & Bar, Gostilna "Pri stričku" — EDI SIMČIČ is another top Brda producer worth adding alongside MOVIA on Day 3 if you have time. Cafe Čokl, PROJEKT, and Pri Stričku are all Ljubljana spots that Jake recommended — good evening wandering options on Day 1 or Day 4 if you want alternatives to the itinerary.
Odprta Kuhna
The open-air food market runs Fridays only. May 29 is a Thursday — you just miss it. The regular Central Market is a fine substitute on any morning.
