Arrive Kobarid · Kozjak Waterfall hike high priority
Easy ~1.5h return hike through a dramatic gorge along the turquoise Soča. The town itself carries Hemingway history — the 1917 Battle of Caporetto retreat he documented in A Farewell to Arms.
Zelenci → Kobarid ~45min
~2 pm
Late lunch: Topli Val high priority
45-year-old seafood institution in the heart of the Alps. Fish from Slovenian Adriatic fishermen, trout from local farms, game from local hunters. Three generations of the Hvala family. Sip Chardonnay on the terrace. Fish carpaccio is the standout.
Late afternoon
Drive the Soča Valley road jake's rec high priority
One of the most scenic drives in Europe — turquoise river through Alpine gorges. Stop at viewpoints. Bovec is ~30 min north if you want to swim in the Soča. Then settle in for the night.
Evening
Dinner: Hiša Polonka jake's rec
Ana Roš's casual sister restaurant to Hiša Franko — same family, same philosophy, much more relaxed and affordable. A great way to ease into Ana Roš's world before the big lunch tomorrow. Right in Kobarid.
Alternative if Polonka is full: Pr'Pepet (also Jake's rec) or grab provisions from a local shop. Either way, keep it lighter than Topli Val lunch — Hiša Franko tomorrow is a 3-hour, €335 marathon.

Hiša Franko ★★★ michelin high priority
Ana Roš's 3-Michelin-star restaurant. 2026 menu "Footprints" — €335 pp, ~3 hours, beverages extra. Spring menu tells the story of the valley through dishes that evolve constantly with what nature provides each week. Watch the Chef's Table episode beforehand if you haven't.
Budget ~3.5 hours including arrival and lingering. Finish around 4–4:30 pm. Wine pairing will add significantly — plan accordingly.
~4:30 pm
Brda wine region winery high priority
~45 min from Kobarid toward Bled. Rolling hills on the Italian border — one of Slovenia's most beautiful landscapes. Three great options for a tasting:
MOVIA — family since 1820, biodynamic, famous Puro sparkling wine and Rebula whites. EDI SIMČIČ (Jake's rec) — another top Brda producer, more boutique feel. Burja Estate (Jake's rec) — natural/minimal intervention style, technically Vipava but nearby. Pick one, maybe two. Call ahead — most require reservations.
Kobarid → Brda ~45min · Brda → Bled ~1h
Evening
Arrive Bled · Dinner: Domačija Majer'ca michelle's rec high priority
Michelle's #1 recommendation — "genuinely one of the best meals, elegant yet homey log cabin, incredibly nice servers." Newish restaurant near Lake Bled, also a guesthouse. Stay here if they have rooms — waking up here after that meal would be a treat.

~20min
Hiša Franko — book immediately

Book lunch on Wednesday May 27. You're already sleeping in the Soča Valley on the night of May 26, so you arrive at the restaurant relaxed, on the right day, in daylight. A farmhouse surrounded by Alpine scenery deserves to be experienced in the afternoon sun, not finished at 10 pm in the dark. The meal ends around 4–4:30 pm, leaving a perfect window to drive through the Brda wine region en route to Bled.

At €335 pp with beverages on top, budget roughly €500–550 pp for the full experience with wine pairing. Book at hisafranko.com — this is the single most time-sensitive booking of the trip.

Where to stay each night

Night 1 (Mon May 25): Ljubljana city center — anywhere walkable to the old town.

Night 2 (Tue May 26): Kobarid or Bovec area. Keep it simple — you're eating lightly and need to be fresh for Hiša Franko the next day. A farmhouse stay or small hotel in Kobarid works perfectly. Agriturismo Nema (Jake's rec, farmstay) is also an option in this region.

Night 3 (Wed May 27): Domačija Majer'ca doubles as a guesthouse — try to book a room there so you're already on-site after dinner. If unavailable, Michelle stayed at Domacija Log v Bohinju (between two mountains, family-owned estate, welcoming host) — also excellent.

Night 4 (Thu May 28): Ljubljana city center again — same area as Night 1, or the same hotel if you want simplicity.

What to book in advance

1. Hiša Franko lunch · Wed May 27 — book today, hisafranko.com

2. Domačija Majer'ca · dinner + room · Wed May 27 — newish, popular, likely fills up

3. Hiša Polonka · dinner · Tue May 26 — Ana Roš's casual sister restaurant, also popular

4. Brda winery · Wed May 27 afternoon — pick one of MOVIA / EDI SIMČIČ / Burja, call ahead, most require reservations

5. TaBar · Mon May 25 and Pr'Stričku · Thu May 28 — worth reservations for three on a weeknight

Didn't make the itinerary — and why
These are great recommendations that simply don't fit a western-focused 4-day loop. Most warrant a return trip.
Ljubljana extras
Strelec, JB Restaurant, Gostilna As, Vigò, Lolita, Pop's Place, Kavarna Zvezda, Museum of Modern Art, MAO, Špajza, Julija, Compa — great for an extra Ljubljana day. Vigò (pistachio ice cream), Lolita (hot chocolate by the river), and Pop's (burgers) are all easy daytime additions on Day 1 or Day 4 if you have time. JB is listed as temporarily closed — verify before planning around it.
East Slovenia
Opok27, Hiša Denk, Terme Olimia, Hotel Laško spa — the Štajerska region is 2+ hours east, completely opposite direction from this route. Both Opok27 and Hiša Denk are exceptional destination restaurants. Hiša Denk has guest rooms from ~$100/night. This region warrants its own dedicated trip (and would pair well with a future Maribor visit).
Southwest Karst
Škocjan Caves, Predjama Castle, Gostilna pri Lojzetu — the Karst/Vipava Valley area is a full day-circuit on its own heading south from Ljubljana. Gostilna pri Lojzetu (17th-century manor, Tomaž Kavčič's deconstructionist Vipava cuisine) is a genuine destination restaurant. Predjama Castle (world's largest cave castle) is spectacular but adds risk to the May 29 morning flight.
Big Pasture Plateau
Velika planina (Jake's rec) — the alpine shepherd plateau is gorgeous but ~1.5h north of Ljubljana in a different direction from Bled/Bohinj. Adding it to Day 4 would mean cutting Bohinj, which is a worse trade. Lake Bohinj + Triglav give you very similar alpine-meadow energy.
Maribor
Maribor is 1h45 east of Ljubljana — a half-day round-trip with no good slot to fit it without sacrificing something major (Bohinj, Soča valley time, or Ljubljana arrival day). Skipping for this trip; a return visit could pair Maribor with Hiša Denk or Opok27 in Štajerska.
